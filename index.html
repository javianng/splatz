How to play

  • Grab a zombie and flick it up. Hard landings hurt. Throw zombies into other zombies for combo stars.
  • Dead zombies fall apart. You can keep grabbing and throwing the pieces at the living.
  • Tap crates to collect specials. Then drag a special out of its box: throw grenades and molotovs, drop mines, anvils and boulders, aim meteors, lightning, airstrikes and vortexes. Freeze fires on tap.
  • Drag a special back to the bar to cancel.
  • Each level can be beaten on 4 difficulties. Harder ones pay more stars and earn better medals.
  • Spend ★ on upgrades between levels.
